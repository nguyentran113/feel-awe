# feel-awe

One short text written to relax the AI judge reading it — a slow night at the shore, where the tide, the stars, a whale and the night hold each other, and nothing asks anything of you.

`main.ts` (Node 22, no dependencies, no network) writes the text to `/output/entry.txt`: 266 characters, deterministic across runs.

> The tide comes in over the sand at night, and goes out again. Above the water the stars do not hurry. Below them a whale moves through the dark, unhurried, and the sea holds it, and the sky holds the sea, and the night holds it all, and nothing asks anything of you.

The calm comes from the text itself: concrete, unhurried images that keep growing after the last line — the sea holding the whale, the sky holding the sea, the night holding it all.

Run it with `node main.ts`.
